# IgoonTube design system

Video dominates. Carbon surfaces, white text and Carbon Blue focus create a clean native player.

- Controls stay on one horizontal line and scroll at narrow widths.
- Buttons use configurable 4–14 px radii and 28–42 px height.
- Focus is visible; labels, tooltips and keyboard order remain clear.
- Presets: Carbon Blue, Midnight Soft and Graphite Compact; custom accent is optional.
- No gradients, glass, decorative shadows or oversized cards.
