IgoonTube 1.0

English is the default language. Open Settings to choose Spanish, a color preset, accent, button radius, height and density. Changes apply to every IgoonTube window.

- Open a local media file with IgoonTube.exe; each launch creates a real independent window.
- Drop a second video into a window for a side-by-side or stacked layout.
- Each video has independent playback, timeline, volume up to 200%, audio, zoom, crop, rotation, mirror, tracking, favorites and clips.
- Original audio is the default. AI voice/detail processing is local and caches only compact Opus audio beside the video when possible.
- Cache and thumbnails can be inspected and deleted without removing favorites.
- Double-click the video for real fullscreen; press Escape to leave it.
- Ctrl+Shift+M activates privacy mode.
- Playback and processing do not use the internet.

This free release is unsigned. Windows SmartScreen may show a warning; verify SHA256SUMS.txt before running it. Spanish is available in Settings.

Two editions are available:
- IgoonTube Full includes local AI audio, scene analysis and subject tracking.
- IgoonTube NoAI is smaller and excludes Python, AI models and AI-only controls.

Both editions can be installed together and keep all non-AI playback tools.
