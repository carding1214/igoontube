# Product

## Platform

Native Windows 10/11 desktop application (.NET 8 WPF, libmpv, FFmpeg and local Python AI runtime).

## Purpose

Private local-media player for one or two simultaneous videos per window, with real independent windows and per-video controls.

## Capabilities

- Local files only; no telemetry, history or network use during playback/processing.
- Independent playback, timeline, 0–200% volume, audio presets, zoom and geometry.
- Side-by-side or stacked two-video layouts; real fullscreen.
- Local subject tracking, scene markers, favorites and automatic timeline thumbnails.
- Local AI voice/detail enhancement with compact reusable audio-only cache.
- Fast A/B clip export, with current-view rendering when requested.
- English by default; Spanish optional. Global Carbon Blue, Midnight Soft, Graphite Compact and custom appearance.
- Per-video/global cache inspection and confirmed deletion; favorites are preserved.
- Unsigned portable package and installer, distributed with SHA-256 hashes.

## Principles

Privacy, compact controls, independent state, immediate playback and reusable heavy processing.
